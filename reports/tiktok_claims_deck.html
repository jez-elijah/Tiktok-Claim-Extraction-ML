Verified authors get fewer views, but only because they post opinions

Text (word pairs) plus duration and author status, fed to logistic regression. Claims lean on words like "read", "learned", "media" and "someone". Opinions lean on "my", "our", "family" and "colleagues".

A perfect score signals templated wording in this dataset. Real speech will be harder, so expect lower accuracy.

Limitations and next steps

  • The transcriptions follow fixed sentence templates, so the model may be learning phrasing rather than what makes something a verifiable claim.
  • Test the model on real, unstructured transcripts, or a hand-labeled sample, before any use in review workflows.
  • Check robustness by masking the obvious cue words and re-scoring.
  • Keep engagement out of the production model, and consider it only for prioritizing already-published videos.
  • Track claim recall, since missed claims are the costlier error for moderation.
